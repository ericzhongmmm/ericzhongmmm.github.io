Passion Fruit Tea Crème Brûlée

Dinner-party custard infused with passion fruit tea under a crackly sugar lid.

Makes 8 ramekins

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups heavy cream
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar, plus 1–2 teaspoons per ramekin for topping
  • 2 passion fruit tea bags
  • 6 egg yolks and 2 whole eggs
  • Pinch of fine salt

Method

  1. Heat the cream, sugar, and tea bags just to a simmer, stirring to dissolve the sugar. Steep for about 8 minutes and remove the bags.
  2. Whisk the yolks, whole eggs, and salt. Strain about half the hot cream into the eggs in a slow stream while whisking, then whisk in the rest.
  3. Divide among 8 ramekins. Cover each loosely with foil and poke a small vent. Set them in a baking dish and pour in hot water to reach halfway up the sides. Bake at 300°F for 30–35 minutes or until the edges are set and the centers wobble slightly.
  4. Remove the foil and let stand in the bath for 5 minutes. Lift the ramekins out, cool, and chill for at least 4 hours. Sprinkle with sugar and torch just before serving.
