Thai-Style Bolognese

Herb-seasoned chicken and pork in a coconut red curry sauce for spooning over rice.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • ½ pound ground chicken and ½ pound ground pork
  • 1 shallot, 2 scallions, and ½ cup cilantro
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce and 2 tablespoons cream
  • 1 can (13–14 ounces) full-fat coconut milk
  • 2–3 tablespoons Thai red curry paste
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables
  • Neutral oil and steamed rice

Method

  1. Blend the shallot, scallions, cilantro, fish sauce, and cream into a paste. Mix it into the meat and form loose clumps or small patties.
  2. Brown the meat in a little oil, then break it into chunky crumbles and transfer to a plate.
  3. Fry the curry paste in a few spoonfuls of thick coconut cream. Stir in the remaining coconut milk and mixed vegetables.
  4. Return the meat and simmer until cooked through to 165°F and the sauce coats it. Taste for seasoning and serve over rice.
