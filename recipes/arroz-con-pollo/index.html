Rice, pasta & noodles

Arroz con Pollo, Eric Style

Cumin-seasoned chicken thighs and tomato rice cooked together in one pan.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin and 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 bell pepper and 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste and ½ teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1½ cups long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 2¼ cups chicken stock or water with chicken bouillon

Method

  1. Pat the chicken dry and season with the cumin and salt. Set it skin-side down in a cold cast-iron skillet and bring it to medium heat. Render the fat until golden, flip to brown the other side, and transfer the thighs to a plate.
  2. Soften the bell pepper, tomatoes, and garlic in the drippings. Stir in the tomato paste and oregano until the paste darkens slightly, then stir in the rice.
  3. Pour in the stock and bring to a simmer. Nestle the chicken back in skin-side up, cover, and cook on low for 20–25 minutes, until the rice is tender and the chicken reaches 165°F. Rest covered for 5 minutes.
