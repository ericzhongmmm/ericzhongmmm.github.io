Steak Diane

A hard-seared steak with mushrooms, shallots, pops of tomato, and a creamy pan sauce.

Serves 2–3

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 large ribeye or strip steak, about 1¼ pounds
  • Kosher salt, black pepper, and 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 2 tablespoons butter and 2 sprigs thyme or rosemary
  • 6 ounces mixed mushrooms, sliced, and 1 shallot, minced
  • ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce and 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon and ⅓ cup heavy cream

Method

  1. Pat the steak dry and salt it generously. Heat the oil in a heavy skillet. Render the fat cap, then sear both sides, flipping often for an even crust.
  2. Add butter and herbs and baste. Cook to 145°F and rest for at least 3 minutes while you make the sauce.
  3. Brown the mushrooms and shallot in the pan. Deglaze with soy sauce and a splash of water, scraping the browned bits, then add tomatoes and Worcestershire.
  4. Stir in Dijon and cream and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon. Slice the steak and spoon the sauce over it.
