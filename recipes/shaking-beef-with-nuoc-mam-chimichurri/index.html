Shaking Beef–Inspired Skirt Steak

Oyster-and-fish-sauce skirt steak with an herby nước mắm chimichurri and fragrant rice.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ pounds skirt steak
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce, 1 tablespoon fish sauce, and 3 smashed garlic cloves
  • ½ cup cilantro, ¼ cup mint, and ¼ cup Thai basil
  • 4 scallions, divided, and 2 extra cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce, 2 tablespoons lime juice, 2 tablespoons water, and 2 teaspoons sugar for the chimichurri
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup neutral oil and 3 thinly sliced garlic cloves
  • 3 cups cooked rice

Method

  1. Coat the skirt steak with oyster sauce, fish sauce, and smashed garlic. Refrigerate for 30 minutes to 4 hours.
  2. Pulse the cilantro, mint, Thai basil, 2 scallions, extra garlic, fish sauce, lime, water, and sugar into a loose herb sauce. Taste and adjust the lime or water.
  3. Caramelize the onion in a little neutral oil. In a separate small pan, gently fry the sliced garlic and the whites of the remaining scallions in the remaining oil. Stir some fragrant oil and fried aromatics into warm rice.
  4. Pat the steak dry and sear in a hot skillet. Cook to 145°F and rest for at least 3 minutes. Slice across the grain and serve with the herb sauce, rice, onions, and scallion greens.
