Sweet Corn Polenta

Creamy polenta made with blended sweet corn and stock.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup frozen corn kernels
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable stock, plus more to loosen
  • 1 cup quick-cooking polenta
  • 2 tablespoons butter, optional
  • ¼ cup Parmesan, optional
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Simmer the corn in 1 cup stock until hot and tender. Blend until smooth, being careful with the hot liquid.
  2. Combine the blended corn and remaining stock in a saucepan and bring to a simmer. Gradually whisk in the polenta.
  3. Cook over low heat, stirring, until creamy according to the package time. Season and finish with butter or Parmesan if using. Loosen with stock just before serving.
