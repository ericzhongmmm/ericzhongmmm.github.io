Dessert

Pumpkin cheesecake

Gingersnap crust and a spoonful of brown sugar–candied sweet potatoes

One 9-inch cake · 12 slices

This is a first-pass recipe. Quantities and times are estimates until I cook the menu.

Ingredients

  • Gingersnap crust1¾ cups finely ground gingersnaps (about 7 oz), 5 tbsp melted unsalted butter, pinch of fine sea salt
  • Pumpkin filling24 oz full-fat brick cream cheese, softened, ¾ cup packed light brown sugar, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 1 cup plain pumpkin purée, ½ cup sour cream, 2 tsp vanilla extract, 1½ tsp pumpkin pie spice, ¼ tsp fine sea salt, 3 large eggs at room temperature
  • Candied sweet potatoes1 medium sweet potato (about 8 oz), peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes, 2 tbsp unsalted butter, ¼ cup packed light brown sugar, 1 tbsp water, ½ tsp ground cinnamon, pinch of fine sea salt

Method

  1. Bake the crust. Heat the oven to 325°F. Line the base of a 9-inch springform pan with parchment. Stir the crumbs, melted butter, and salt together and press evenly into the base. Bake for 10 minutes and let cool while you mix the filling.
  2. Mix the filling. Beat the cream cheese, brown sugar, and cornstarch on low until smooth. Mix in the pumpkin, sour cream, vanilla, spice, and salt. Add the eggs one at a time on low, stopping as soon as each is incorporated.
  3. Bake and chill. Pour the filling over the crust and set the pan on a rimmed baking sheet. Bake at 325°F for about 55–75 minutes, until the edges are set, the center still jiggles slightly, and a thermometer in the center reads 160°F. Cool on a rack for about 1 hour, then refrigerate for at least 6 hours, ideally overnight. Cover once chilled.
  4. Candy the sweet potatoes. Heat the oven to 400°F. Melt the butter and mix it with the brown sugar, water, cinnamon, and salt in a small baking dish. Toss with the cubes. Bake for 20–30 minutes, stirring halfway, until fork-tender and glossy. Cool before topping the cheesecake.
  5. Finish. Unmold the chilled cheesecake and top with the candied sweet potatoes. For clean slices, dip a knife in hot water and wipe it dry between cuts.
