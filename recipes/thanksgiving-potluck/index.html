A holiday menu by Eric Zhong

My Thanksgiving potluck.

I’m planning duck two ways this Thanksgiving. The breast gets crispy skin, pumpkin polenta, and a spoonful of tart cranberries. The confit goes in a salad with harissa sweet potatoes. I’m saving a few sweet potatoes for the cheesecake, too.

These are my first-pass recipes. Quantities and times are estimates until I cook the menu.

  • MainPan-seared duck breastPumpkin polenta, roasted kale, mushroom demi-glace, and cranberry compote→
  • SideAgrodolce duck confit saladHarissa sweet potatoes, bitter greens, feta, pickled onions, and roasted cashews→
  • DessertPumpkin cheesecakeGingersnap crust and a spoonful of brown sugar–candied sweet potatoes→
Make-ahead plan ↓

Potluck prep

The day before

Bake and chill the cheesecake. Make the candied sweet potatoes, cranberry compote, mushroom demi-glace, pickled onions, and dressing. Refrigerate each in its own container. Wash and dry the greens.

On the day

Roast the harissa sweet potatoes, cook the polenta, and reheat the sauce. Roast the kale and sear the duck close to serving. Pack the toppings and dressing separately, then dress the salad and top the cheesecake at the table.

For transport and serving, keep hot foods at 140°F or warmer and cold perishables at 40°F or colder. Discard perishables after 2 hours at room temperature, or after 1 hour above 90°F. See the federal buffet guidance and cooking temperature chart.
