Side

Agrodolce duck confit salad

Harissa sweet potatoes, bitter greens, feta, pickled onions, and roasted cashews

8 side servings

This is a first-pass recipe. Quantities and times are estimates until I cook the menu.

Ingredients

  • Glazed duck confit2 fully cooked duck confit legs, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, 2 tbsp maple syrup, 1 tbsp water
  • Harissa sweet potatoes1¼ lb sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into ¾-inch cubes, 1½ tbsp olive oil, 1–2 tbsp harissa paste to taste, ½ tsp fine sea salt
  • Pickled onions½ red onion, thinly sliced, ¼ cup apple cider vinegar, ¼ cup hot water, 1 tsp sugar, ½ tsp fine sea salt
  • Honey herb vinaigrette¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tsp honey, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 2 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley, 1 tbsp chopped chives, salt and black pepper to taste
  • Greens and toppings3 cups thinly sliced stemmed kale, 3 cups baby arugula, 2 cups torn radicchio, ½ cup crumbled feta, ½ cup roasted cashews, roughly chopped

Method

  1. Quick-pickle the onion. Stir the vinegar, hot water, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Pour over the sliced onion and let stand at least 20 minutes. Drain before serving.
  2. Roast the sweet potatoes. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the cubes with oil, harissa, and salt. Roast on a sheet pan for 25–30 minutes, turning once, until browned and tender. Let cool slightly.
  3. Glaze the confit. Remove the meat and skin from the bones and tear into bite-size pieces. Crisp in a skillet over medium heat and heat the duck to 165°F. Transfer to a plate. Pour off excess fat, then simmer the vinegar, maple syrup, and water in the pan until lightly syrupy, about 2–4 minutes. Return the duck and toss briefly to coat.
  4. Whisk the vinaigrette. Mix lemon juice, honey, and Dijon. Whisk in the olive oil, then stir in parsley, chives, salt, and pepper.
  5. Toss just before serving. Massage the kale with a spoonful of dressing. Add the arugula, radicchio, and enough remaining dressing to lightly coat. Scatter over the sweet potatoes, warm glazed duck, drained onions, feta, and cashews.
