Main

Pan-seared duck breast

Pumpkin polenta, roasted kale, mushroom demi-glace, and cranberry compote

6 main servings or 8 tasting portions

This is a first-pass recipe. Quantities and times are estimates until I cook the menu.

Ingredients

  • Duck breast3 boneless, skin-on duck breasts (10–12 oz each), 1 tsp fine sea salt, ½ tsp black pepper
  • Pumpkin polenta1 cup medium-ground polenta, 3 cups low-sodium chicken stock, 1 cup whole milk, 1 cup plain pumpkin purée, 2 tbsp unsalted butter, ½ cup finely grated Parmesan, salt to taste
  • Roasted kale1 bunch lacinato kale, stemmed and torn, 1 tbsp olive oil, ¼ tsp fine sea salt
  • Mushroom demi-glace8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 minced shallot, 1 tsp thyme leaves, ½ cup low-sodium chicken stock, ½ cup prepared demi-glace, 1 tbsp cold unsalted butter
  • Cranberry compote8 oz fresh or frozen cranberries, ⅓ cup sugar, ¼ cup orange juice, ¼ cup water, 1 tsp finely grated orange zest, pinch of salt

Method

  1. Simmer the compote. Cook the cranberries, sugar, orange juice, water, zest, and salt over medium heat for 10–12 minutes, until most berries burst and the juices look syrupy. It thickens as it cools.
  2. Make the polenta. Bring the stock and milk to a gentle simmer. Whisk in the polenta in a steady stream. Cook on low, stirring often, for 25–35 minutes or until tender. Stir in the pumpkin and cook 5 minutes more. Finish with butter, Parmesan, and salt. Loosen with warm stock if needed.
  3. Roast the kale. Heat the oven to 350°F. Dry the leaves well, toss with oil and salt, and spread in a single layer. Roast for 10–15 minutes, checking at 10, until the edges crisp.
  4. Reduce the sauce. Brown the mushrooms in the oil over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes. Add shallot and thyme and stir for 1 minute. Pour in the stock and demi-glace and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon, about 5–10 minutes. Swirl in cold butter off the heat.
  5. Sear the duck. Pat dry. Score the skin in a shallow crosshatch, keeping the cuts in the fat. Season both sides and set the breasts skin-side down in a cold, heavy skillet. Use two skillets if needed to keep them flat and uncrowded. Set over medium-low heat and render for about 12–18 minutes, carefully spooning off excess fat, until the skin is deep golden and crisp. Flip and continue cooking over medium heat until the thickest part registers 165°F. Rest 5 minutes, skin-side up, then slice across the grain.
  6. Plate. Spoon the polenta onto a warm platter and lay the sliced duck on top. Spoon the demi-glace around the duck to keep the skin crisp. Add cranberry compote and roasted kale just before serving.
