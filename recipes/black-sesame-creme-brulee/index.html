Black Sesame Crème Brûlée

Toasted black sesame makes a nutty custard under a thin caramel shell.

Makes 4 ramekins

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup black sesame seeds
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • ⅓ cup granulated sugar, plus 1–2 teaspoons per ramekin for topping
  • Pinch of fine salt

Method

  1. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan until fragrant. Grind or blend them with the sugar and salt until fine, adding a spoonful of the cream if needed to make a paste.
  2. Heat the remaining cream to a gentle simmer. Whisk yolks into the sesame mixture, then slowly whisk in the hot cream. Strain if you want a smoother finish.
  3. Divide among four ramekins. Cover loosely with vented foil and set in a baking dish. Pour in hot water to reach halfway up the ramekins and bake at 300°F for about 35–45 minutes, until the edges set and the centers still wobble slightly.
  4. Let stand in the bath for 5 minutes. Lift out, cool, and refrigerate at least 4 hours. Blot the tops, sprinkle with sugar, and torch just before serving.
