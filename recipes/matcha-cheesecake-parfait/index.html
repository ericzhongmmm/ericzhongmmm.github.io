Matcha Cheesecake Parfait

A no-bake matcha cheesecake layer with crushed biscuits and ice cream.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 2 teaspoons matcha powder, sifted
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ¾ cup crushed biscuits or shortbread
  • 4 small scoops vanilla ice cream

Method

  1. Beat the cream cheese with matcha and honey until smooth. Whip the cream separately to soft peaks and fold it into the matcha mixture.
  2. Layer the crushed biscuits and matcha cheesecake in four glasses. Chill until ready to serve.
  3. Add a scoop of ice cream to each glass just before serving. Finish with a pinch of crushed biscuits or matcha.
