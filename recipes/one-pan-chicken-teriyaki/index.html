One-Pan Chicken Teriyaki

Rendered chicken thighs with a sticky soy glaze and sautéed vegetables.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 4 skin-on, boneless chicken thighs
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 5 ounces shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage or coleslaw mix
  • 1 cup bean sprouts and 3 scallions
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 2 tablespoons rice vinegar, 2 tablespoons sugar, and ¼ cup water

Method

  1. Season the chicken and start it skin-side down in a cold skillet. Bring to medium heat, render until the skin browns, flip, and transfer to a plate.
  2. Brown the mushrooms in the drippings. Toss in cabbage, sprouts, and scallions until just tender. Season and finish with sesame oil, then set the vegetables aside.
  3. In the same pan, stir together soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, and water. Return the chicken and juices and simmer until the thighs reach 165°F and the sauce is sticky.
  4. Serve the glazed thighs with the vegetables and rice if you like.
