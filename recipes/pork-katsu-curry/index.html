Pork Katsu Curry

Crisp pork cutlets over rice with a quick Japanese curry sauce.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pork tenderloin, cut into 4 pieces and pounded thin
  • Kosher salt
  • 2 eggs, ¼ cup flour, and 1–2 tablespoons water for batter
  • 1½ cups panko and neutral oil for frying
  • 1 onion, 1 bell pepper, and 1 carrot, diced
  • 2 cups chicken broth and 2–3 Japanese curry roux cubes
  • Steamed rice

Method

  1. Soften the onion, pepper, and carrot in a little oil. Add broth and simmer until tender. Stir in curry roux and simmer until smooth and thick enough to spoon.
  2. Salt the pounded pork. Whisk eggs and flour with just enough water to make a thin batter. Coat each cutlet in the batter, then press into panko.
  3. Fry in hot oil around 340°F until crisp and the pork reaches 145°F. Drain on a rack and rest at least 3 minutes.
  4. Slice and serve over rice with curry spooned alongside to keep some crust crisp.
