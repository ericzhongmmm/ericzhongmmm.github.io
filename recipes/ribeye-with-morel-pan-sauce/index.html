Ribeye with Morel Pan Sauce

A ribeye with roasted mushrooms, shallot, Worcestershire, and Dijon.

Serves 2–3

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 large ribeye, about 1¼ pounds
  • Kosher salt, black pepper, and 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • ¾ ounce dried morels or 6 ounces fresh mushrooms
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • ½ cup stock or carefully strained mushroom soaking liquid
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce and 1 teaspoon Dijon
  • 2 tablespoons cold butter

Method

  1. If using dried morels, soak them in hot water until pliable. Rinse away grit, strain the soaking liquid through a fine filter, and drain the mushrooms.
  2. Pat the steak dry, season, and sear in hot oil. Cook to 145°F and rest for at least 3 minutes.
  3. Brown the mushrooms and shallot in the steak pan, cooking the mushrooms thoroughly. Deglaze with stock or soaking liquid and reduce. Stir in Worcestershire and Dijon.
  4. Take off the heat, swirl in cold butter, and spoon over the sliced steak.
