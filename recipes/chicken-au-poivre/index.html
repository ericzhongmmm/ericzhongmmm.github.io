Roast Chicken au Poivre

Spatchcocked chicken with mustard butter and a peppery pan sauce.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 4 pounds, spatchcocked
  • 4 tablespoons softened butter, 1 tablespoon Dijon, and 1 minced garlic clove
  • Kosher salt and 2–3 teaspoons cracked black pepper, divided
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • ¼ cup dry white wine or brandy
  • ½ cup chicken stock and ½ cup heavy cream

Method

  1. Pat the chicken dry and season generously with salt. Mix the butter with Dijon and garlic, then rub it under the skin and over the bird. Season with cracked pepper.
  2. Roast on a rimmed pan at 425°F until the thickest part of the breast and thigh reaches 165°F, about 40–55 minutes. Transfer the chicken to rest.
  3. Sauté the shallot and remaining pepper in a little of the pan fat. Deglaze with wine or brandy and reduce, then add the stock and cream. Simmer until the sauce lightly coats a spoon.
  4. Carve the rested chicken and spoon the pepper sauce over it.
