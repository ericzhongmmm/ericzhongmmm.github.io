Garlic Mashed Potatoes

Yukon Gold potatoes mashed with hot garlic cream and butter.

Serves 4–6

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into even chunks
  • Kosher salt
  • ¾ cup heavy cream
  • 3 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 5 tablespoons unsalted butter, cubed

Method

  1. Put the potatoes in cold, generously salted water. Bring to a simmer and cook until easily pierced, about 15–20 minutes.
  2. Warm the cream with the garlic while the potatoes cook. Steep for 10 minutes and remove the garlic.
  3. Drain and briefly steam-dry the potatoes. Mash or rice them, stir in butter, and add hot cream a little at a time until smooth. Taste and adjust the salt.
Kitchen note

A potato ricer gives a smooth mash with very little stirring.
