Skillet Shakshuka

A small-skillet tomato and egg dish with garlic-scented toast.

Serves 2–3

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 shallot, sliced, and 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 cups chopped fresh tomatoes
  • ¼ cup chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • Kosher salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes to taste
  • 4 large eggs
  • 4 thick slices bread and 1 garlic clove, halved

Method

  1. Warm olive oil in a skillet and soften the shallot and garlic. Add the tomatoes and cook until they break down and release their juices.
  2. Add the stock and let it reduce. Stir in tomato paste, season, and simmer until thick enough to hold a well.
  3. Make four wells and crack in the eggs. Cover and steam gently until the whites and yolks are set to your liking. Use pasteurized eggs if serving runny yolks.
  4. Toast the bread, rub it with the cut garlic, and serve alongside the skillet.
