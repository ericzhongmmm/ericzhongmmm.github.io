Chicken Parm, but Lighter

Grilled chicken paillard with blistered tomatoes, a bright tomato-onion salad, and fresh mozzarella.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 large chicken breasts, split into 4 thin cutlets
  • Kosher salt, black pepper, and 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, divided
  • ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup basil leaves
  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar and a pinch of oregano
  • 6 ounces fresh mozzarella, torn

Method

  1. Pound the chicken cutlets to an even thickness. Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning and rub with oil.
  2. Halve half the cherry tomatoes. Toss with the onion, basil, vinegar, oregano, and a little oil and salt. Let sit while the chicken cooks.
  3. Blister the remaining whole tomatoes in a hot pan with olive oil and a pinch of salt. Crush a few to make a quick sauce.
  4. Grill or pan-sear the chicken until it reaches 165°F. Serve with blistered tomato sauce, the marinated salad, and torn mozzarella.
