Broccoli Rabe & Asparagus Pesto Pasta

A bright greens pesto folded into penne with soft, golden zucchini.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces penne
  • 2 zucchini, cut into pieces about the size of the penne
  • 1 small bunch broccoli rabe, ½ bunch asparagus, and 1 cup baby spinach
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan or vegetarian hard cheese
  • ½ cup olive oil, divided
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the zucchini in 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium heat until deeply tender and golden. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Blanch the broccoli rabe and asparagus in salted water until bright and tender. Add spinach for the last few seconds. Cool, squeeze dry, and roughly chop.
  3. Blend the greens with lemon zest and juice, cheese, remaining oil, and salt. Boil the penne in the blanching water and save a cup of the pasta water.
  4. Finish the pasta with the zucchini and a splash of pasta water. Take the pan off the heat and fold in the pesto. Loosen with more pasta water as needed.
Kitchen note

Use a rennet-free hard cheese for a vegetarian version or omit the cheese for a plant-based version.
