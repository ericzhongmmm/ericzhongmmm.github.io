Chicken Piccata

Thin chicken cutlets with capers, lemon, and a butter-mounted pan sauce.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts, split and pounded into 4 thin cutlets
  • ⅓ cup flour, kosher salt, and black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ¼ onion or 1 shallot, minced, and 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 tablespoons drained capers
  • ¼ cup dry white wine and ½ cup chicken stock
  • 3 tablespoons cold butter and chopped parsley

Method

  1. Season the cutlets and lightly dredge in flour. Sear in hot olive oil until golden on both sides, then remove to a plate.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in the same pan. Add capers, wine, stock, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Simmer until reduced.
  3. Return the cutlets and cook through to 165°F. Lower the heat and whisk in the cold butter a piece at a time, keeping the sauce below a boil. Finish with parsley and serve with pasta if you like.
