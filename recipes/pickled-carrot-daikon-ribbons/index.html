Pickled Carrot & Daikon Ribbons

Sweet, sharp carrot and daikon ribbons for a shared platter or sandwich.

Makes about 3 cups

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 carrots and ½ medium daikon, peeled
  • ⅓ cup rice vinegar and ⅓ cup distilled white vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1½ teaspoons kosher salt

Method

  1. Shave the carrots and daikon into ribbons with a peeler or spiralizer.
  2. Whisk the vinegars with sugar and salt until dissolved. Taste the brine and adjust the sweetness to your liking.
  3. Toss with the ribbons and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, tossing once. Serve cold with grilled meat, rice, or sandwiches.
Kitchen note

This is a refrigerator pickle.
