Pumpkin & Boursin Agnolotti

Fresh filled pasta with pumpkin, Boursin, and Parmesan in sage brown butter.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 300 g 00 or all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 3 large eggs, 1 teaspoon olive oil, and a pinch of salt
  • Optional squid ink for half the dough, about 1 teaspoon
  • 1 cup pumpkin purée, blotted dry
  • 1 package (5 ounces) Boursin and ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • 5 tablespoons butter and 10 sage leaves
  • Kosher salt and pasta water

Method

  1. Mix flour, eggs, oil, and salt into a dough. For two colors, make half the dough separately with squid ink whisked into its eggs. Knead, wrap, and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Blend pumpkin, Boursin, and Parmesan into a thick filling. Transfer to a piping bag or a zip-top bag with a corner snipped.
  3. Roll the dough into thin sheets. Pipe a line of filling, fold the dough over, pinch into small pillows, and cut between the pinches. Press out air and seal well.
  4. Boil the agnolotti in salted water for 2–3 minutes. Brown the butter with sage and add a spoonful of pasta water. Gently toss with the pasta and finish with Parmesan.
