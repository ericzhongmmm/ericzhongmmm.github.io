Rigatoni Amatriciana

A pantry-friendly take with bacon, garlic, shallot, and a silky tomato sauce.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces rigatoni
  • 5 ounces thick-cut bacon or guanciale, diced
  • 1 shallot and 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1 can (14–15 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • ½ cup grated Pecorino Romano
  • Kosher salt and reserved pasta water

Method

  1. Boil the rigatoni until just shy of done. Save 1 cup pasta water.
  2. Brown the bacon in a wide skillet. Add shallot, garlic, and pepper flakes and cook until fragrant.
  3. Add tomatoes and simmer for 10–15 minutes. Transfer the rigatoni into the sauce with a splash of pasta water and toss until glossy. Finish with Pecorino and check the salt.
