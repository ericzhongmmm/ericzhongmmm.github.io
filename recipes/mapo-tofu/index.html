Mapo Tofu with Extra Pork

Soft tofu in a numbing Sichuan pepper and pork sauce with shiitake.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 block (14–16 ounces) soft tofu, cut into cubes
  • ¾ pound ground pork
  • 1½ teaspoons Sichuan peppercorns
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • 4 shiitake mushrooms, sliced, and 3 scallions, whites and greens separated
  • 3 cloves garlic and 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 2 tablespoons doubanjiang and 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons cool water
  • Steamed rice

Method

  1. Toast and grind the Sichuan peppercorns. Brown the pork in neutral oil and cook it through to 160°F.
  2. Add mushrooms, scallion whites, garlic, ginger, and ground peppercorns. Stir-fry briefly, then fry the doubanjiang until fragrant.
  3. Add the stock and simmer for several minutes. Stir in the slurry and bring to a brief boil until the sauce thickens.
  4. Gently fold in the tofu and let it warm through. Finish with scallion greens and serve over rice.
