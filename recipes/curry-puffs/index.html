Pork & Potato Curry Puffs

Flaky puff pastry filled with ground pork, potatoes, onion, and Japanese curry roux.

Makes 12 puffs

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • ½ pound ground pork
  • 1 small potato and ½ onion, finely diced
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • ½ cup water or chicken stock
  • 1–2 Japanese curry roux cubes, about ¾–1½ ounces
  • 2 sheets thawed puff pastry
  • 1 egg beaten with 1 tablespoon water

Method

  1. Brown the ground pork in oil, then stir in the onion and potato. Add stock and simmer until the potato is tender and the pork reaches 160°F.
  2. Stir in the curry roux until dissolved and cook until the filling is thick enough to mound. Cool completely.
  3. Roll the pastry to an even thickness and cut into 12 squares or rounds. Add a small scoop of filling, fold over, and press or crimp the edges. Brush with egg wash.
  4. Bake at 400°F for 18–22 minutes, until puffed and golden. Cool briefly before serving.
