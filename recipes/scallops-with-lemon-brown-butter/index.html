Scallops with Lemon Brown Butter

Hard-seared scallops with garlic, anchovy, lemon zest, and a buttery pan sauce.

Serves 2–3

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound dry sea scallops
  • Kosher salt and 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced, and 1 teaspoon anchovy paste
  • ¼ cup dry white wine or stock
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • ½ teaspoon honey, optional

Method

  1. Pat the scallops thoroughly dry. Salt just before adding to a very hot oiled skillet. Sear until golden on one side, then flip and add a knob of butter. Cook until the scallops are firm and opaque, then transfer to a plate.
  2. Soften garlic in the butter left in the pan. Stir in anchovy paste and let the butter take on a nutty color. Deglaze with the wine or stock and reduce briefly.
  3. Take the pan off the heat and whisk in the remaining cold butter, lemon zest, and juice. Add a touch of honey if you like. Spoon over the scallops and serve right away.
