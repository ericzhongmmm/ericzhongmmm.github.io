Bacon-Wrapped Manchego Dates

Sweet dates, crisp bacon, and pockets of nutty Manchego.

Makes 16 bites

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 16 Medjool dates, pitted
  • 4 ounces Manchego, cut into small batons
  • 8 slices thin-cut bacon, halved crosswise
  • 1–2 tablespoons honey, optional
  • 16 toothpicks

Method

  1. Slit each date and tuck in a baton of Manchego. Wrap each in half a slice of bacon and secure with a toothpick.
  2. Bake seam-side down at 400°F for 16–22 minutes, turning once, until the bacon is browned and crisp.
  3. Brush lightly with honey if you like and return to the oven for a minute or two. Let cool briefly before serving.
