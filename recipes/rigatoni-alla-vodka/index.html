Rigatoni alla Vodka

Rigatoni in a silky tomato-vodka sauce with crisp bacon, shallot, and Parmesan.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces rigatoni
  • 4 ounces thick-cut bacon, cut into lardons
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 shallot and 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • ⅓ cup vodka
  • 1 cup crushed tomatoes or passata
  • ¾ cup heavy cream and ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • Kosher salt and pasta water

Method

  1. Boil the rigatoni in generously salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water.
  2. Brown the bacon in olive oil. Add the shallot, garlic, and pepper flakes and cook until fragrant.
  3. Take the pan briefly off the flame to add vodka, then return to heat and reduce until almost dry. Stir in the tomatoes and simmer until slightly thickened, then stir in the cream.
  4. Toss the pasta into the sauce with a splash of pasta water. Finish stirring over medium-low heat until the sauce coats each piece, then fold in Parmesan and adjust the salt.
