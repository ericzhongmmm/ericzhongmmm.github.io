Pork Shoulder Birria Quesatacos

Tender pork shoulder, chile broth, and cheese in a crisp tortilla for dipping.

Makes 10–12 tacos

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds boneless pork shoulder, cut into chunks
  • 4 dried guajillo chiles and 2 dried ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • ¼ onion and 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon dried oregano, and 1 bay leaf
  • 2 tablespoons cider vinegar and 3 cups broth
  • Neutral oil and kosher salt
  • 10–12 corn tortillas and 2 cups grated Oaxaca cheese or low-moisture mozzarella
  • Chopped onion, cilantro, and lime

Method

  1. Toast the chiles briefly, then soak in hot water until pliable. Blend with onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, vinegar, and 1 cup broth. Season the sauce with salt.
  2. Season and brown the pork in a heavy pot. Add the chile sauce, remaining broth, and bay leaf. Cover and braise at a low simmer until the pork shreds easily, about 2½–3 hours.
  3. Shred the pork and reserve the broth for dipping. Brush tortillas with some of the fat from the surface and fill with cheese and pork.
  4. Griddle until crisp on both sides. Serve with onion, cilantro, lime, and small bowls of broth.
