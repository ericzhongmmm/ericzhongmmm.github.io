Caramelized Shallot Beurre Blanc

A wine-and-vinegar reduction whisked with cold butter for fish or chicken.

Makes about ¾ cup

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 large shallots, minced
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil or pan drippings
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 1 tablespoon red or white wine vinegar
  • ½ cup cold unsalted butter, cut into cubes
  • Kosher salt

Method

  1. Cook the shallots in oil or drippings over medium-low heat until softened and lightly caramelized.
  2. Add wine and vinegar. Scrape up any browned bits and reduce until only 1–2 tablespoons of liquid remain.
  3. Turn the heat very low or take the pan off the burner. Whisk in the cold butter a cube at a time. Stop when the sauce is glossy and coats a spoon.
  4. Season with salt and serve immediately over fish or chicken.
Kitchen note

If the sauce starts to look oily, take it off the heat and whisk in a teaspoon of cold water.
