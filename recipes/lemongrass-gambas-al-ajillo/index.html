Vietnamese-Inspired Gambas al Ajillo

Shrimp in warm lemongrass, scallion, and garlic oil for sharing.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • ⅓ cup olive oil or neutral oil
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, trimmed and bruised
  • 4 scallions, whites and greens separated
  • 5 garlic cloves, roughly minced
  • Pinch of chile flakes and kosher salt
  • Lime and bread or rice, to serve

Method

  1. Bruise the lemongrass with a mallet. Warm the oil with lemongrass, scallion whites, and a pinch of chile flakes over low heat until fragrant.
  2. Strain the infused oil over the garlic, then return the oil and garlic to the skillet. Warm until the garlic sizzles without browning deeply.
  3. Pat the shrimp dry, season with salt, and cook in the oil until firm and opaque, about 1–2 minutes per side.
  4. Scatter sliced scallion greens over the shrimp and serve with lime and bread or rice to soak up the oil.
