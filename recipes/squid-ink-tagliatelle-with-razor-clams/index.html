Squid Ink Tagliatelle with Razor Clams

Homemade squid ink noodles with razor clams, cherry tomatoes, and garlic.

Serves 3–4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 225 g all-purpose or 00 flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 2 large eggs and 1–2 tablespoons squid ink
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 anchovy fillets or 1 tablespoon anchovy paste
  • 4 cloves garlic, coarsely chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tins razor clams, with a little of their packing liquid
  • Chopped parsley, lemon, and salt, to taste

Method

  1. Whisk the squid ink into the eggs and mix with the flour. Knead until smooth, wrap, and rest for 30 minutes. Roll thin, dust between layers, and cut into tagliatelle.
  2. Warm the oil with the anchovy, breaking it up until it dissolves. Add garlic, then the tomatoes and a splash of clam liquid. Cook until the tomatoes soften.
  3. Boil the fresh pasta in salted water for about 2 minutes. Transfer to the sauce with a splash of pasta water and toss until glossy.
  4. Fold in the razor clams just long enough to warm them. Finish with parsley and lemon.
