Cantonese Egg Fried Rice

Egg-yolk-coated rice fried until golden with browned onion and a little sesame oil.

Serves 2–3

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice, cooled or straight from a ready-to-heat pouch
  • 3 large eggs, separated
  • ½ onion, finely diced
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • Kosher salt and sliced scallions

Method

  1. Break up the rice with your fingers. Toss it with the yolks and a pinch of salt. Lightly beat the whites separately with another pinch of salt.
  2. Heat a wok or skillet until hot. Add the oil and fry the onion until the edges brown, then add the rice.
  3. Spread and toss the rice until it separates and turns golden in spots. Make a well, add the whites, and let them set briefly before tossing through.
  4. Finish with sesame oil, scallions, and salt to taste.
