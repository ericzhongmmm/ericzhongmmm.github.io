Brown Butter Miso Peanut Butter Cookies

Chewy brown butter cookies with salty miso and a scoop of peanut butter.

Makes 12 cookies

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda and a pinch of fine salt
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar and ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • ¼ cup smooth peanut butter and 1 tablespoon white miso
  • 1 large egg and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt. Brown the butter in a small pan, stirring until the milk solids are golden and nutty.
  2. Take it off the heat and whisk in peanut butter, miso, and both sugars. Cool until warm, then whisk in the egg and vanilla.
  3. Fold wet into dry. Chill for 20–30 minutes if the dough is too soft to scoop. Divide into 12 balls and space them well apart on parchment-lined trays.
  4. Bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are set and the centers still look soft. Let them finish setting on the tray.
