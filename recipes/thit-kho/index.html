Thịt Kho with Eggs

Vietnamese caramelized pork belly braised with aromatics and eggs.

Serves 4–6

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1½ pounds skinless pork belly, cut into chunks
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar, divided
  • 1 onion, sliced, and 5 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1 inch ginger, sliced, and 3 scallions, whites and greens separated
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce and 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, peeled
  • Black pepper and steamed rice

Method

  1. Heat 2 tablespoons sugar in a heavy pot over medium heat until it turns amber. Carefully add the pork and stir to coat, then let it render and brown.
  2. Add onion, garlic, ginger, scallion whites, fish sauce, and soy sauce. Stir until the onion softens.
  3. Pour in broth, add remaining sugar if needed, and simmer covered until the pork is tender, about 60–90 minutes. Add the peeled eggs for the final 15 minutes.
  4. Uncover and reduce to the consistency you like. Finish with scallion greens and black pepper, and serve over rice.
