Shrimp Chawanmushi

Silky dashi egg custard steamed gently and topped with shrimp and sesame oil.

Makes 4 cups

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1½ cups prepared dashi, cooled
  • 1 teaspoon light soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon mirin, or ½ teaspoon rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar
  • 8 small peeled shrimp
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil and fine salt

Method

  1. Beat the eggs gently with soy sauce and mirin, then stir in the cooled dashi. Strain into four heatproof cups and skim away any bubbles.
  2. Set the cups in a covered steamer over gently simmering water. Steam over low heat for about 15 minutes, until mostly set.
  3. Toss the shrimp with a pinch of salt and a little sesame oil. Lay them on the custards and steam for about 5 more minutes, until the shrimp are opaque and the custard is set. Finish with sesame oil.
