Bacon & Egg Congee

A quick bowl of instant-rice congee with crisp bacon, white pepper, and chili crunch.

Serves 2

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked instant rice
  • 3 cups chicken stock, plus more to loosen
  • 4 strips bacon, chopped
  • ½ onion, finely diced
  • ½ teaspoon toasted sesame oil and ¼ teaspoon white pepper
  • 2 pasteurized egg yolks, or 2 fully cooked eggs
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce and 2 teaspoons chili crisp
  • Sliced scallions

Method

  1. If using pasteurized yolks, place them in the soy sauce with a little chili crisp and refrigerate while you make the congee. Otherwise, prepare the cooked eggs for topping.
  2. Render the bacon in a saucepan until crisp. Lift out half for garnish. Soften the onion in the drippings and add sesame oil, stock, and white pepper.
  3. Bring to a boil and stir in the cooked rice. Simmer, stirring and breaking up the rice, until creamy, 10–15 minutes. Add stock if it tightens and season to taste.
  4. Top each bowl with bacon, scallions, chili crisp, and an egg.
