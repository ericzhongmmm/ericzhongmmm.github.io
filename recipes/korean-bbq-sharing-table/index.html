Korean BBQ Sharing Table

A tabletop spread of thin-sliced meats, griddled kimchi, and ssamjang for dipping.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • ¾ pound thin-sliced beef belly or short plate
  • ¾ pound thin-sliced beef brisket
  • ¾ pound thin-sliced pork belly
  • 2 cups napa cabbage kimchi and 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • ¼ cup ssamjang
  • 1 tablespoon each soy sauce, honey, and water for a quick glaze
  • 2 teaspoons sesame oil and a pinch of salt
  • Steamed rice and lettuce leaves, to serve

Method

  1. Stir together the soy sauce, honey, and water. Set out the ssamjang and a small dish of sesame oil with a pinch of salt.
  2. Heat a tabletop griddle or cast-iron skillet. Cook the beef belly in batches, brushing it with glaze in the last moments. Sear the brisket with a pinch of salt and cook the pork belly until browned and cooked through.
  3. Griddle the kimchi and garlic in a little rendered fat. Toss a batch of cooked brisket with some of the kimchi.
  4. Set everything on the table with rice and lettuce so people can make their own bites.
