Spaghetti Carbonara

Bacon and black pepper in a silky egg-and-cheese sauce.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces spaghetti
  • 5 ounces bacon or guanciale, cut into lardons
  • 2 large eggs and 2 egg yolks
  • ¾ cup finely grated Pecorino Romano or a Pecorino-Parmesan mix
  • 1 teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper
  • Kosher salt and reserved pasta water

Method

  1. Brown the bacon in a skillet and keep the rendered fat. Whisk eggs, yolks, cheese, and black pepper in a mixing bowl.
  2. Boil the spaghetti until al dente and save 1 cup pasta water. Toss the hot pasta with the bacon and some of its fat.
  3. Take the pan off the heat. Toss in the egg mixture quickly, adding small splashes of pasta water until the sauce is glossy and clings to the noodles. Finish with more black pepper and cheese.
