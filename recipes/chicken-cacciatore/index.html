Chicken Cacciatore

Skin-on thighs braised with mushrooms, peppers, and tomatoes in one pot.

Serves 4–6

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • Kosher salt, black pepper, and 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 small onions and 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced, and ½ cup dry white wine
  • 1 can (28 ounces) whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano and a pinch of red pepper flakes

Method

  1. Season the thighs. Brown them skin-side down in a Dutch oven until they render fat, then flip and brown the other side. Move them to a plate.
  2. Brown the mushrooms in the drippings. Add onions, bell pepper, and garlic and cook until softened. Pour in wine and scrape up the fond.
  3. Crush in the tomatoes by hand, add oregano and pepper flakes, and bring to a simmer. Nestle in the chicken and any juices. Cover and simmer 25–35 minutes, until the thighs reach 165°F and the sauce is rich.
  4. Taste for salt and serve with bread, rice, or polenta.
