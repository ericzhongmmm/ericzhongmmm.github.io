Beef Satay Tacos

Charred steak, coconut-peanut sauce, and a lime-heavy cucumber slaw in warm tortillas.

Makes 8 tacos

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ pounds flap or skirt steak
  • For the marinade — 1 tablespoon Thai red curry paste, 1 teaspoon curry powder, 1 tablespoon honey, and 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  • For the slaw — 1 large cucumber, ½ red onion, ½ cup cilantro, 1 Fresno chile if you like heat, juice of 3 limes, and kosher salt
  • For the sauce — ½ cup coconut milk, ¼ cup smooth peanut butter, 1 tablespoon Thai red curry paste, 1 tablespoon honey, and 1–2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • Neutral oil, kosher salt, and 8 small tortillas

Method

  1. Mix the curry paste, curry powder, honey, and vinegar. Coat the steak and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 4 hours.
  2. Dice the cucumber, slice the onion and Fresno chile, and chop the cilantro. Toss with lime juice and salt. Taste for a bright, assertively tart slaw.
  3. Warm coconut milk, peanut butter, and curry paste in a small pan and whisk until smooth. Add honey and soy sauce. Thin with a splash of water if needed.
  4. Pat the steak dry, salt both sides, and char it in a hot cast-iron skillet with a little oil. Cook to 145°F and rest at least 3 minutes. Slice thinly across the grain.
  5. Warm the tortillas. Spread each with peanut sauce, add steak, and pile the slaw on top.
