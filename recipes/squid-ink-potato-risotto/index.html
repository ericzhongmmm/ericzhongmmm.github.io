Squid Ink Potato Risotto

Little cubes of russet potato cooked risotto-style, finished with squid ink and garlicky shrimp.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1½ pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cut into even ¼-inch cubes
  • 3 cups warm seafood or chicken stock, plus more as needed
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¾ pound peeled shrimp
  • 1–2 teaspoons squid ink
  • ¼ cup panko, kosher salt, and lemon wedges

Method

  1. Warm the stock. Toast the potato cubes in 1 tablespoon olive oil for a few minutes. Add a ladle of stock and stir until it is mostly absorbed.
  2. Keep adding warm stock a ladle at a time, stirring to release the potato starch. Stop when the cubes are tender and surrounded by a creamy sauce.
  3. In a separate skillet, briefly cook the garlic in the remaining oil, then add shrimp and cook until firm and opaque. Toast the panko in a little oil.
  4. Stir the squid ink into the potatoes, season, and serve with the shrimp, panko, and lemon.
