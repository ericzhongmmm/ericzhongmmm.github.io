Lemongrass, Mint & Basil Simple Syrup

A bright aromatic syrup for iced tea, sparkling water, and cocktails.

Makes about 1¼ cups

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water and ¾–1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, trimmed and cut into pieces
  • ¼ cup mint leaves and ¼ cup basil leaves

Method

  1. Bruise the lemongrass with a mallet or the flat side of a knife. Gently crush the mint and basil to release their aroma.
  2. Combine water, sugar, and the aromatics in a saucepan. Warm while stirring until the sugar dissolves, then simmer gently for 3–5 minutes.
  3. Turn off the heat, steep for about 15 minutes, and strain. Cool and refrigerate. Add a spoonful to your drink and adjust to taste.
