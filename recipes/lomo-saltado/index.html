Lomo Saltado

Seared steak strips and bell peppers in a glossy, sharp oyster-and-soy sauce.

Serves 4

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ pounds steak, cut into strips
  • 2 bell peppers and 1 red onion, sliced
  • 4 scallions, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce, 3 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce, and 2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 2 cloves garlic and 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil, salt, and black pepper
  • Hot fries and steamed rice, to serve

Method

  1. Whisk oyster sauce, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and ginger. Pat the steak dry and season lightly.
  2. Sear the beef in a hot, oiled skillet in batches so it browns. Remove it as you go.
  3. Stir-fry the peppers and onion until crisp-tender. Add tomatoes and scallions, then pour in the sauce and reduce briefly.
  4. Return the beef and toss until coated. Spoon over rice with hot fries on the side or toss the fries in right before serving.
