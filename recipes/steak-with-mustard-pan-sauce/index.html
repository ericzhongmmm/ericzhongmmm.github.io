Steak with Mustard Pan Sauce

A seared steak with shallots, Dijon, and whole-grain mustard in a quick pan sauce.

Serves 2–3

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 large strip steak or ribeye, about 1 pound
  • Kosher salt and 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 2 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 2 shallots, minced
  • 2 teaspoons Dijon and 2 teaspoons whole-grain mustard
  • ½ cup chicken stock
  • Black pepper and a splash of water as needed

Method

  1. Salt the steak generously and sear in a very hot oiled cast-iron pan. Add 1 tablespoon butter and baste. Cook to 145°F and rest for at least 3 minutes.
  2. Soften the shallots in the steak pan. Deglaze with the stock and a splash of water, scraping up the browned bits.
  3. Whisk in both mustards and reduce until the sauce coats a spoon. Swirl in the remaining butter off heat. Taste, then serve over sliced steak.
