Quick Miso Cod

A short miso-and-honey marinade gives seared cod a deeply browned glaze.

Serves 2

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets, about 6 ounces each
  • 2 tablespoons white miso
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon mirin or water
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil

Method

  1. Whisk miso, honey, vinegar, and mirin until smooth. Coat the fish and refrigerate for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Remove the fish from the marinade and wipe off the excess so the glaze browns evenly. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Sear on both sides, watching closely because the honey browns quickly. Cook to 145°F or until opaque and flaking. Serve with rice or greens.
