Tuna Melts with Cornichons

Olive oil–packed tuna, crunchy cornichons, and a crisp cheese edge.

Makes 2 sandwiches

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (5 ounces each) tuna in olive oil, mostly drained
  • 1 small shallot, finely minced
  • 5 cornichons, sliced into small rounds
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon whole-grain mustard and a dash of hot sauce
  • 4 slices sturdy bread, 4 slices cheddar, and 2 tablespoons softened butter

Method

  1. Mash the tuna with shallot, cornichons, mayo, mustard, and hot sauce until the mixture holds together. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  2. Spread tuna salad on two slices of bread and top with cheese. Close the sandwiches and butter the outside of both slices.
  3. Griddle over medium heat until both sides are crisp and the cheese melts. Let any cheese that escapes crisp up in the pan before serving.
