Egg Foo Young with Gravy

Golden omelet patties with a glossy savory gravy.

Makes 4 patties

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup bean sprouts or shredded cabbage
  • 3 scallions, sliced, and ½ small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil and ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce and 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons cool water

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with sesame oil and salt. Fold in the vegetables.
  2. Heat a skillet with a thin layer of oil. Ladle in small patties and fry until golden on both sides and set all the way through.
  3. Simmer the stock, soy sauce, and oyster sauce. Whisk in the slurry and let it bubble until glossy. Spoon over the patties just before serving.
