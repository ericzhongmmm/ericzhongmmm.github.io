Brown Butter Pumpkin Bread

Toasted flour, reduced pumpkin, and brown butter make a deeply nutty loaf.

Makes 1 large loaf

Watch the recipe

Watch on YouTube ↗

Measurements are estimates for home cooking. Season to taste.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 can (15 ounces) pumpkin purée
  • ½ cup unsalted butter and ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1½ cups packed brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
  • 2 large eggs and 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda, ½ teaspoon baking powder, and ½ teaspoon fine salt

Method

  1. Toast the flour in a dry skillet over medium-low heat, stirring until it smells nutty and turns pale gold. Cool and whisk in baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Cook the pumpkin purée in a separate pan until slightly darker and thick enough to mound. Brown the butter, then stir in the oil, brown sugar, and spice off the heat.
  3. Stir the pumpkin into the butter mixture and let it cool to warm. Whisk in eggs and vanilla, then fold in the dry ingredients just until combined.
  4. Scrape into a parchment-lined 9-by-5-inch loaf pan. Bake at 325°F for 65–80 minutes, until a tester comes out with a few moist crumbs. Cool before slicing.
